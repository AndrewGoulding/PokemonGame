# Unova interactive music

This imports the original Black/White ISS sound tables and connects shared B2/W2 musicians to the original sequence parts. It does not supply the missing BW2-exclusive music archive.

- Village Bridge: Derleth’s whistle, Aickman’s guitar, Russo’s beatbox and Koontz’s vocals can each be started/stopped. Their source masks are preserved, including grouped tracks. The bridge houses preserve the arrangement; leaving the sound area resets it. Reloading inside the area preserves the saved arrangement.
- Accumula: the new source musicians’ house includes the pianist, drummer and Minccino. Show a non-Egg Kricketot, then a non-Egg Whismur, for one Metronome. The pianist plays after the quest; the drummer can play immediately. Once playing, they continue until the player leaves the sound area. Full bags preserve the earned reward for a later claim.
- Nacrene’s accordion, Icirrus’s dancers, Anville’s flute and the versioned postgame Opelucid musician control the source proximity parts. The source descending distance/volume tables and product of axis volumes are used, centered on the authored 2D actors. DS world coordinates and heights remain in provenance; horizontal distances are projected to native map tiles. Icirrus does not reproduce the DS dance choreography.

`node tools/import-unova-iss.js /path/to/pokeblack /path/to/pokebw2` reads NARCs `a/1/4/7` and `a/1/3/2`, checks the source nine-switch layout, and writes masks, fade times, zone banks and emitter profiles with archive hashes/commit IDs. The Subway camera-dependent switch set and Driftveil Gym’s 3D height-dependent emitter remain recorded but unsupported. `tools/lib/unova-music-layers.js` imports source actor models and script-entry messages and registers the new room.

The BW audio importer now retains original SSEQ track IDs. The renderer can isolate track masks without skipping their tempo/control events. A dedicated worker synthesizes aligned stems at the existing 32,728 Hz rate. Playback adjusts GainNodes without restarting the song; newly prepared stems join at the current playhead. The retained PCM omits the redundant first complete loop but retains the intro and warmed-up looping body. Village Bridge’s five stems fit within 128 MiB; ordinary music cache entries are evicted against the same budget. Leaving a music family releases its retained buffers. Fanfares pause and restore all prepared parts together. Sound/mono controls continue to operate through the shared master output.

This is browser stem playback rather than DS streaming synthesis: isolated stems have independent voice allocation, and the renderer’s existing per-stem limiting can differ from the original hardware’s combined mix. Unsupported-worker browsers use the same renderer on the main thread.

Validation: source masks/track IDs, audible and silent PCM, aligned loop boundaries and retained size; source quest ordering, Eggs, full bags, saved completion and area resets; native browser musician toggles, uninterrupted song clock, five-part playback, jingle restoration, Accumula quest/duet, city distance fading and save/reload. Run `npm test` and `tests/browser/unova-music-layers.js`.
