# Castelia and the Kalos cities

The twelve street plans built by `tools/build-city-layouts.js`. Each was compared with the picture on the page
listed (looked at, not copied) and the builder was corrected where it disagreed. "Corrected" means the things
named in the notes now stand on the side of town the reference shows; none of these plans is a traced map, and
the notes say what still differs. Doors can only face south in this builder, and every map kept its size.

| Map | Reference | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `UnovaCasteliaCity` | https://www.pokewiki.de/Stratos_City (whole-city map, drawn for Black/White, plus the page's Black 2/White 2 text and its pier/street table) | corrected | Pier order was wrong: Unity Pier was the easternmost and the sewer hatch sat on the promenade by the second pier. Now west to east Liberty, Unity, Prime (ferry), Cruise Dock, and a short Thumb Pier with the sewer entrance (the page puts it on the easternmost jetty). Buildings were on the wrong streets: Battle Company moved from the north-west to the waterfront facing Prime Pier; GAME FREAK from the centre to Castelia Street's east side; Passerby Analytics HQ from the north-east to the waterfront at the foot of Gym Street; the Medal Office to Gym Street's west side (the page: "in Gym Street"); Café Sonata next to Narrow Street; the Pokémon Center from east of Castelia Street to between Mode Street and Castelia Street. Iris now waits by the Thumb Pier. Already matching: Gym in the west between Gym Street and Narrow Street, Route 4 gate due north of the fountain plaza, Skyarrow Bridge gate at the east end, Prime Pier in the middle. Still differs: the streets are a grid, not a fan, and the plaza is too far north; the whole-city picture is the Black/White one, so Black 2/White 2 details come from the page's text only; the ice-cream stand, Studio Castelia and the avenue north of the plaza are not drawn; the Badge Office, Dream Lab and street rooms are not in the real city. `tests/city-layouts-test.js` pinned the Center east of x=40; that assertion now expects it between the two eastern streets, and new assertions pin the pier order. |
| `KalosLumioseSouth` | https://www.pokewiki.de/Lumiose_City (city plan with coloured plazas and gates; South Boulevard render) and https://www.pokewiki.de/S%C3%BCdring | corrected | Vert and Bleu Plazas were swapped: Bleu is south-west of the tower, Vert south-east. Café Soleil is on South Boulevard. Still differs: in the game the lab and Center face each other across the boulevard (here side by side); the city is a rounded rectangle; Estival Avenue and the canal street are not drawn. The test's Route 5 assertion (`x < 5`) was updated to south-west. |
