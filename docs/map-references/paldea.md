# Paldea map references

Plans: `tools/plans/paldea.js` (towns, Team Star bases, Area Zero stations, Kitakami and Blueberry places),
`tools/plans/paldea-routes.js` (routes and wild areas).

Scarlet/Violet is a 3D open world, so there are no tile maps. Three kinds of reference were used and looked at
(none is stored here):

- **Overview screenshots** from pokewiki.de (one per town; the page is listed in the table under its German
  name: Mesagoza = Mesalona City, Cabo Poco = Cucharico, Los Platos = Pratolido, Cortondo = Moldrid,
  Artazon = Bolardin, Cascarrafa = Garrafosa City, Levincia = Fermanca City, Medali = Mesclarra,
  Montenevera = Frigomonta, Alfornada = Asarilla, Mossui Town = Midoriya).
- **Location screenshots** from serebii.net's Pokéarth (one per location, ground level).
- **The in-game region map**, top-down, from Pokéarth's map tiles (`serebii.net/pokearth/paldea/`,
  `.../kitakami/`, `.../terarium/`, 4096 px across). It shows each town's footprint, streets, roof colours,
  Pokémon Centers, water and cliffs, and was the main source for arrangement. Referred to below as "the map".

Every map is built on a fixed frame that cannot move: towns have gates/doors at about 19,4 · 4,17 · 27,10
(some also 10,26 · 24,14 · 24,15) and a cross of streets to the middle of each side; routes have the way in at
17,28 and the way on at 17,3. So "corrected" means the terrain, landmarks, building style and colours were made
to agree with the reference around those points; a landmark whose real position is taken by a fixed door is noted.

Verdicts: 60 maps compared — 29 matches, 29 corrected, 2 differs, 0 without a reference.

## Gyms, League and battle rooms

Plans: `tools/plans/paldea-rooms.js`. Each of these maps is one battle place (a Leader, Elite or boss stands on
the court; the only door leads back to the town or cave). In Scarlet/Violet the Gym buildings are identical
reception lobbies and the Leader is fought on a court outdoors in the town (Medali's is inside the Treasure
Eatery), so each Gym map was judged as that town's battle court. References: pokepedia.fr's Gym pages (one
screenshot of each court, "Terrain de Combat", plus the lobby and the building), serebii.net's Gym, Elite Four,
Blueberry Elite Four and Area Zero Underdepths pages (cut-scene screenshots), Pokéarth location shots and the
Terarium map. pokewiki.de has no pages for Paldea's Gyms. Fixed in every map: the way in at 12,19 and the
Leader's tile.
