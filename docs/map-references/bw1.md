# Unova (Black / White campaign, `BW1*` maps)

Plans: `tools/plans/bw1.js` (towns), `tools/plans/bw1-routes.js` (routes and outdoor areas). All reference pages are on
pokewiki.de (German names). Every `BW1` map is a 48x40 grid whose gates, doors and people are fixed, usually with gates
north/south or west/east through the middle and the Pokémon Center at the lower left; the real maps are long strips or
L-shapes with gates elsewhere, so "corrected" means the landmarks, their relative positions, ground and roof colours now
follow the reference as far as those fixed points allow. Bulbapedia was not used. Cave maps (`BW1WellspringCave` and the
other eight caves) were not compared.

## Gyms and League rooms

Plans: `tools/plans/bw1-rooms.js`. Scope is the 8 maps of `node tools/plan-preview.js list BW1 gym` (there are only
eight: Striaton to Opelucid; the League rooms are not in that list and were not compared). References are the Gym
interior renders on pokewiki.de's "Pokémon-Arena von …" pages (Black/White pictures). Every one of these Gyms is the
same open 44x34 hall here with the Leader at the top and three switches in a row, so the real Gym's layout cannot be
followed; what was compared is floor and wall colours, the set-piece and what stands where. Solid props standing on
open floor with nothing solid in the reference were removed or painted flat.
