# Alola map references

Plans: `tools/plans/alola.js` (towns and landmarks), `tools/plans/alola-routes.js` (routes and wild areas).

Alola is a 3D region, so there are no tile maps. The references used are pokewiki.de's whole-area map
renders (the guidebook-style overhead pictures on each location page) and its island map art. They were
compared for arrangement, landmarks, terrain and colours. Nearly every map here is built on a fixed frame
(a gate in the north at about 19,4, one in the west at about 4,17, a door at about 27,10, and rim openings
in the middle of each side), so compass directions often cannot match the real place; that is noted as
"differs" or in the notes.

## Trial sites and League rooms

Plans: `tools/plans/alola-rooms.js`. References: pokewiki.de's guidebook renders of each trial site and of the League chambers (Sun/Moon versions), plus in-game screenshots; serebii.net's screenshots for Team Rainbow Rocket's castle. The five "entrance" maps are forecourts that do not exist in the games (the captain's gate there is outdoors), so they were compared with the first area of the real site for terrain and colour only. Every warp and person is fixed, which is why the Totems and League members stand where they do.
