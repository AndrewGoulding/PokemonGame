# Kalos (Pokémon X/Y) map references

X/Y are 3D games, so the references are overhead map renders (pokewiki.de `XY_<place>.png`, which show a whole
town or route from above) and, where noted, single screenshots (serebii.net Pokéarth). They were compared for
arrangement, landmarks, terrain and colours; nothing was copied. Doors, gates, people and existing water are fixed in
our maps, so several places keep a different orientation from the real one — the Notes say which.

Note on names: on pokewiki.de Dendemille Town is `Frescora` and Couriway Town is `Mosaia` (`Fluxia` is Anistar,
`Fractalia` is Snowbelle, `Batika` is Kiloude).

## Gyms and League rooms

References here are in-game screenshots, not overhead renders: one or two angled 3D views per Gym
(serebii.net Pokéarth `maps/kalos/<n>-gym.jpg` and `xy/gym/gym<n>.jpg`, pokepedia.fr's Gym pages) and one view per
League chamber (pokepedia.fr `Chambre_…_XY.png`, serebii.net `xy/elite/elite<n>.jpg`). pokewiki.de answered 403 on this
pass. A screenshot shows colours, materials and the set-piece well but only part of the room, so where a Gym is
several maps here (canopy levels, quiz floors, doll rooms, starfields, ice halls) the same picture was used for each.
These rooms keep the game's walkable shape, so only floor kinds, colours, the painted set-piece and props were changed.
