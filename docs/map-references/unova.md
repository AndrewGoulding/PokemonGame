# Unova (Black 2 / White 2) map references

Plans: `tools/plans/unova.js` (towns) and `tools/plans/unova-routes.js` (routes and outdoor areas).
References are the whole-map renders on pokewiki.de (file names `S2W2_…` = Black 2/White 2, `SW_…` = Black/White)
and, where pokewiki has none, the Pokéarth screenshots on serebii.net. Pictures were looked at only; none is stored here.

What can never match: every door, gate, person and sign of our maps is fixed, and so is water that was already on
the map. Our maps also have their own sizes (most towns are 44x40 tiles), so a town's proportions and the exact
position of a building are ours; what was compared is orientation, water, cliffs, street pattern, which landmark
is where, how many buildings there are and their colours, and the ground.
