# Platinum optional challenges and collection rewards

The Seven Stars Restaurant has nine source trainer couples, five daily tables,
09:00–23:00 opening hours and native two-opponent double battles. The Mansion’s
Five-Maid Knockout uses its five source Clefairy teams, a daily 5–9-turn target
and the exact-turn Blissey challenger. Its Rare Candy is a held item: bring
Thief or Covet. Leaving between matches preserves the challenge; reloading an
unfinished battle forfeits that day’s attempt. Fight Area adds Barry’s tag
battle against Flint and Volkner. The shared tag engine uses three Pokémon
per trainer, with Barry’s Staraptor, Snorlax and fully evolved starter.

Other quests include Route 221’s exact-level requests and cycling held-item
prizes, Pastoria’s two-gender Combee reward, source IV/personality-based
Remoraid size records, the lost Suite Key and White Flute, Mr. Fuego’s Star
Piece/Shard exchanges, the Mansion Soothe Bell and Manaphy book, and Battle
Tower’s 20/50/100-win Underground trophies. Gifts check capacity before
recording completion. Daily schedules use the game’s saved walking clock.

Old Chateau now has all nine source-linked rooms, source encounter tables,
painting and disappearing apparitions. Snowpoint Temple has all six floors,
source stair connections and native ice movement, with Regigigas’s existing
quest at the bottom. Fuego Ironworks has an accessible interior, source
workers/items and native spinning floor tiles. Room layouts and puzzles are
explicit 2D adaptations. Existing Rotom/Regigigas map IDs and quest flags are
retained. A local cartridge selector at Pal Park enables source Ruby,
Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen encounter slots after National Dex
certification; it simulates the DS’s GBA slot without another device.

Sinnoh’s Start-menu Trainer Card uses Platinum’s six source palettes and
five completion stars. Its Badge Case supports keyboard or pointer polishing,
daily dirt and badge tones with source pitch intervals. Diploma artwork is
imported from Platinum. The Director awards the Sinnoh Diploma at 210 seen
and the National Diploma at the source 482 required caught species, including
Platinum’s original eleven exclusions. Tower trophies, Master Contest wins,
50 Underground flags, National completion and the Champion feed the card’s
collection records. Badge tones use a synthesized local instrument.

Validation covers daily rotation, battle recovery, rewards, source teams,
held-item theft, sizes, dual-slot encounters, all Temple ice routes, source
warp destinations, five-star records and save reload. The browser test drives
native NPC rewards, Trainer Card/Badge Case controls, persistent polishing,
diploma display and descent through the six ice floors.
