# Kalos · X/Y campaign and postgame

Speak to the **Kalos researcher in Sinnoh’s Fight Area**, beside the Unova researcher, and choose Pokémon X or Y. The campaign starts at home in Vaniville. Travel guides in Vaniville and South Lumiose return to Fight Area; Kalos remembers its position and healing spot. Existing Kalos saves continue from their current progress.

The playable story now reaches **Diantha, AZ’s ending and the main postgame quests**, with **221 maps and 348 trainer/partner records**, plus **208 normal and 864 Super Maison Pokémon sets**. It follows the opening through Viola, South Lumiose and Sycamore’s lab, Route 5 and Camphrier, Parfum Palace, Snorlax and western Route 7, Connecting Cave, upper Route 8 and Ambrette, Route 9 and Glittering Cave, then the Aquarium/coastal path to Cyllage. The journey continues through Geosenge, Reflection Cave, Shalour, Route 12 and Azure Bay, Coumarine, Route 13 and the Power Plant, then northern Lumiose. The newest arc adds Laverre, the Poké Ball Factory, Dendemille/Frost Cavern, Anistar, Lysandre Labs and the Flare headquarters, Couriway, Snowbelle, Pokémon Village and Victory Road. The Town Map covers these destinations. The League, Kiloude and the Looker case are playable.

## Playable events

- Choose Chespin, Fennekin or Froakie from Tierno, battle Shauna’s weaker starter, deliver Sycamore’s letter to Mom, and receive the Town Map. Route 2’s catching demonstration is narrated and gives ten Balls; it does not put a demonstration capture into your party or Pokédex. Austin guards progress into the forest. Shauna heals there; meet the friends near the northern exit, battle Rinka for Roller Skates, then follow the spiderweb to Viola. The Bug Badge raises the cap from 12 to 25. Viola awards Infestation, and Alexa awards Exp. Share, recognizing an already-owned copy.
- Route 4 has flower encounters, its original field trainer rosters and Dexio’s Return gift. South Lumiose includes a Center, Sycamore’s three-floor lab and Café Soleil; northern Lumiose opens after the Power Plant rescue. Battle Sycamore’s three Lv. 10 Kanto starters, choose a Lv. 10 Bulbasaur/Charmander/Squirtle, and receive its Mega Stone. Charmander’s stone follows X/Y. There are also Luxury Balls and False Swipe. Meet Lysandre and Diantha through the café scene. The Holo Caster is supplied automatically when entering/resuming Kalos, matching its original starting ownership; its local Use/Select function reviews Sycamore’s research and the next story goal. Existing Mega Rings from other regions are retained; Korrina grants Kalos’s Mega Ring later at the Tower of Mastery.
- Meet Korrina on Route 5, battle Tierno’s Corphish, and receive Trevor’s Honey. Route 5 has source Gulpin/Scraggy hordes and the rare mixed group: four Plusle/one Minun in X, four Minun/one Plusle in Y. Use Honey from the Bag in Route 5’s grass/flowers, or encounter hordes through the existing 1-in-20 land check. Honey is consumed only when it can attract a group. Kalos horde Pokémon have a 5% per-member Hidden Ability roll, a project sampling rule. Other new routes currently use regular encounter tables; their horde pools remain future work.
- Visit Shabboneau Castle, investigate Snorlax, and pay the palace’s one-time ¥1,000 admission. In the gardens, place Shauna at the entrance, drive Furfrou north, then have her guard the east lane and approach Furfrou from its west. Wrong approaches reset the chase. Watch an animated balcony fireworks scene, receive Protect and borrow the Poké Flute. Cut is available in the gardens. Wake the Lv. 15 Snorlax with its original moves and Sitrus Berry; capture or defeat clears the bridge. Running or losing permits a retry. The caretaker retains the borrowed flute afterward, without taking a separate Poké Flute already owned for another region.
- Join Calem against Trevor and Tierno in a native Multi Battle, with a source partner team chosen from your Kalos starter. Cross Connecting Cave and upper Route 8. Ask the Ambrette Fossil Lab about its assistant, then mount Rhyhorn on Route 9 and press A at the two blocking rocks to smash them. Leaving Route 9 dismounts automatically; the entrance sign also allows dismounting. Cleared rocks remain saved. The ride uses native grid movement, an existing Rhyhorn follower sheet and the shared player sprite, rather than X/Y’s 3DS animation or Circle Pad controls.
- Defeat two Team Flare Grunts, then join Calem against two independent Flare opponents in a second native partner battle. Rescue the scientist and choose Jaw Fossil or Sail Fossil. Ambrette restores it to a Lv. 20 Tyrunt or Amaura when there is a free party slot; a full party does not consume the fossil. Receive Rock Smash and, after the rescue, Aerodactylite. The Aquarium supplies this project’s shared fishing rod and opens the coastal path to Cyllage.
- Get the Bicycle at Cyllage’s cycle shop, meet Grant on the racetrack for Strength, and climb four visible wall transfers to reach him. His Lv. 25 Amaura and Tyrunt use their source moves and Refrigerate/Strong Jaw. Winning awards the Cliff Badge, Rock Tomb, Strength’s field permission, and a cap of 32. Four original Gym students are present. Centers in Lumiose, Camphrier, Ambrette and Cyllage support healing, storage, shopping and regional respawns.


- Clear three Team Flare encounters on Route 10, investigate Geosenge’s stones with Shauna and battle Korrina’s two Lucario. Reflection Cave has a multi-floor route, a concealed mirror passage to Shadow Ball, Flash and a rest stop. In Shalour, meet Gurkinn and battle Calem, then defeat four Roller Skaters to raise the rails to Korrina. Her Mienfoo, Machoke and Hawlucha award the Rumble Badge and Power-Up Punch; the cap becomes 34.
- Climb the Tower of Mastery for the Mega Ring and a Lucario-versus-Lucario tutorial. Press START in the move menu to activate Mega Evolution. Winning without using it permits a retry. The borrowed Lucario temporarily replaces the party; the original party, including HP, PP and held items, is restored after the battle or a reload. Lucario’s optional Lv. 32 gift retains Lucarionite. A full party leaves the gift waiting without blocking onward travel.
- Receive Surf from Calem, adopt the optional Lv. 30 Lapras and cross Route 12’s water channel. Ride Skiddo at Baa de Mer Ranch and press A at its fence opening to leap across for treasures. Azure Bay has water encounters, Ampharosite and the quiet Sea Spirit’s Den. Meet Sycamore and Diantha for Fly at Coumarine’s Seaside Station, then take the monorail to Hillcrest. Battle Calem and climb two rope-course canopies to Ramos. His Jumpluff, Weepinbell and Gogoat award the Plant Badge, Grass Knot, Fly permission and a cap of 37.
- Find the hidden Power Plant Pass by Route 13’s bridge boulder and defeat its nearby Flare Grunt. Clear eight plant Grunts, the Admin and Aliana to restore power, with healing and saved rewards. Northern Lumiose opens; meet Bonnie and answer four silhouette quizzes in Prism Tower. Wrong answers require their Trainer battle and allow another choice. Clemont’s Emolga, Magneton and Heliolisk award the Voltage Badge, Thunderbolt and a cap of 42. Meet Sycamore and Lysandre at the café for a King’s Rock and the invitation to Route 14. New Centers support healing, storage, shopping and respawns.


- Battle Calem on Route 14 and join your friends for the Scary House tale. Laverre Gym has nine dollhouse rooms, reciprocal yellow warp panels and a green return panel. Valerie’s source Mawile, Mr. Mime and Sylveon award the Fairy Badge, Dazzling Gleam and a cap of 48. In the Poké Ball Factory, reverse the belt control, clear the Grunts/Admin and join Calem against Bryony’s Liepard and Celosia’s Manectric in a native Multi Battle. The president supplies a Master Ball and Big Nugget, with saved capacity-safe reward retries.
- Route 15 leads through autumn ruins to Dendemille. Route 16 and the Lost Hotel are optional branches. Frost Cavern has several floors and native slippery ice. Defeat the Flare Grunt and Mable’s Houndoom to rescue Abomasnow and receive Abomasite. Mamoswine then carries you across Route 17; press A at its boulder to smash it, with the cleared rock saved and automatic dismounting on departure. Battle Calem in Anistar and cross the Gym’s star paths to Olympia. Her Sigilyph, Slowking and Meowstic award the Psychic Badge, Psychic and a cap of 59.
- Enter the secret lab through Lysandre Café. Fight Lysandre, explore the transfer-pad hall and scientist wings, take Mable’s Elevator Key, hear AZ’s account in his cell and defeat Xerosic. Either weapon-console button leads to the weapon’s activation, as in X/Y. Return to Geosenge, battle Lysandre again, join Calem against three separate Flare pairs, and let Shauna open the legendary chamber. Four Admin battles free Lv. 50 Xerneas in X or Yveltal in Y. Capture is mandatory; defeat, running and cancellation leave a retry. A full party sends it to the PC, and a full party plus full PC prevents starting the encounter. Both are shiny-locked, have at least three perfect IVs, and retain the source story moves. Capture reconciliation survives reload before the scene finishes.
- Defeat Lysandre’s final team, including Lv. 53 Mega Gyarados, and escape to Geosenge. A reload during the aftermath also moves you outside the destroyed headquarters. Continue through Route 18, the optional upper Terminus Cave floors and Couriway’s Sycamore battle. Shauna, Tierno and Trevor challenge you consecutively on Route 19’s bridge; Trevor heals first. Their Waterfall gift is saved and retryable.
- Find Wulfric in Pokémon Village after traversing four Winding Woods clearings. Snowbelle Gym’s four buttons save platform orientations modulo four; pink/yellow align at 1 and blue/green at 3, opening two physical bridges. Wulfric’s Abomasnow, Cryogonal and Avalugg award the Iceberg Badge, Ice Beam, Waterfall permission and the pre-League cap of 68. Cross Route 21 and five Victory Road areas, including a required Surf crossing, rest stop and Calem’s final pre-League battle. The League entrance opens after all eight badges and that rival battle; the Elite Four challenge is now open.


- Register at the League entrance and challenge Malva, Siebold, Wikstrom and Drasna in any order. Each chamber locks you to its current opponent until victory; losing or leaving the challenge resets its four victories. Diantha uses her original six Pokémon and Mega Gardevoir. Winning records a separate Kalos Hall of Fame entry and raises the cap to 100. The entry is recorded once even if a save interrupts the victory scene. Read the records at the League entrance attendant or Hall of Fame room. Rematches use the same X/Y teams and levels.
- Attend Lumiose’s parade, receive the Honor of Kalos and battle AZ’s three Lv. 60 Pokémon. Either battle outcome completes the ending. AZ reunites with Floette, and you return home. Mom or the League attendant can return you to a pending parade if you leave early. Sycamore, Sina and Dexio supply the National Pokédex and TMV Pass at Lumiose Station. The train runs both ways to Kiloude, which has a Center, a strategy house with Anton’s source team, a station quiz, Vs. Recorder gift and the Battle Maison.
- The Maison offers Normal and Super Singles (three Pokémon), Doubles (four), Triples (six), Rotation (four) and Multi (two personal Pokémon plus a CPU partner). Multi partners are Shauna, Tierno and Trevor with their source X/Y teams. Each course has its own streak/best record and shares BP. Normal win 20 faces the corresponding Chatelaine and unlocks that format’s Super course. Super win 50 faces the stronger Chatelaine team and leaves the streak open indefinitely. Multi uses two Chatelaines, with two Pokémon each. Normal milestones award Skillful Battler Ribbons; Super milestones award Expert Battler Ribbons and a lobby monument per format.
- Maison levels cap down to 50; lower levels remain unchanged. Species and held items must differ; Eggs, Shadow Pokémon, Soul Dew, restricted legends and Pokémon introduced after Generation VI cannot enter. Only Mega Evolution is available among battle gimmicks. There is no Bag use, experience, prize money, blackout or opponent Pokédex registration. Temporary teams preserve the real party’s HP/PP/items/status. Interrupted battles restore the real party and forfeit only the interrupted course’s streak. Teams may be reselected between battles without resetting a streak. The records attendant can retire individual courses. Normal BP is 1 per win through 10, 2 through 19 and 20 at win 20. Super BP rises from 2 to 6 in ten-win blocks, awards 50 at win 50, then 7 per win thereafter. BP buys the displayed prizes.
- After participating in the Maison, battle Calem on Kiloude’s northern hill once per game day. His six-Pokémon team includes Mega Absol; the first win awards Absolite. Report Mega Evolution research to Sycamore at Anistar’s sundial to open the Looker case. Timed Mega Stone hunts have been removed; regional stone placement is planned separately. Shauna’s Lv. 5 starter trade and Diantha’s Modest Lv. 5 Ralts holding Gardevoirite accept any eligible party Pokémon, with confirmation before exchanging it. These trades are one-time and preserve party capacity.
- Zygarde waits beyond Terminus’s eastern stairs and final chamber, with four source treasure rewards. Its encounter is shiny-locked, guarantees three perfect IVs and uses source moves. Escaping permits another attempt; defeating it requires another Hall of Fame entry before it returns. Full storage prevents starting a capture. Kalos’s legendary bird tracking and Mewtwo encounter have been removed because those Pokémon are available in Kanto. Previously caught Pokémon and collected items are retained in old saves. The empty dungeon and stone-hunt rooms remain accessible so saved locations remain valid.
- After reporting the research, visit the Looker Bureau near Rouge Plaza. Find five tickets in the Center, Prism Lobby, Herboriste, hotel and museum; investigate the children’s hideout; choose **Dance** to befriend Mimi; rescue the visitor’s stolen Pokémon; inspect the museum painting and follow three disguised thieves. Meet Chalmers and Malva, enter the hidden lab, defeat six guards and read all five Expansion Suit notes. Four consecutive Essentia battles expose Xerosic’s experiment. Looker’s letter, the museum and the café complete the farewell. Emma can then be challenged once on each Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday of the game clock. The bureau retains your chapter, evidence, battles and rematch day across saves.

Roller Skates toggle from the Key Items pocket or Select after registering them. They enable running-speed movement outdoors in Kalos, using the shared walking/running animation. Analog tricks are not implemented. Skating suspends indoors, while surfing and outside Kalos. Rhyhorn and Mamoswine have mounted displays and saved rock-clearing interactions. Skiddo has its native fence jump. The factory control, dollhouse panels, lab transfers, forest clearings and ice-platform bridges are authored 2D puzzle adaptations; they do not interpret the original 3DS machinery or model rotations.

## Shared collection, independent journey

Progress lives in `flags.kalos`, with `Kalos…` maps, `XY_…` trainer flags and `kalos-…` badges. Party, boxes, money, Bag and Pokédex remain shared. Other regional starters and story flags are retained. Starter/wild moves, battle level-up learning and Rare Candy learning use X/Y level-up data while in Kalos. Battle records carry only the participating species’ source learnsets. Kalos has its own healing destinations and field-move badge requirements. A location recovery pass moves saves out of newly solid geometry, including Route 4’s new hedge beds. Viola’s web return and the new canyon/Gym exits have explicit working warp behaviors.

## Sources and rebuilding

- `tools/data/kalos-xy.json` is an offline snapshot of veekun/pokedex at `cc483e1877f22b8c19ac27ec0ff5fafd09c5cd5b`: 721 ordered X/Y level-up learnsets and 122 versioned encounter datasets. These include walking/rough terrain, distinct flower surfaces, cave slots, and coastal Surf/rod pools. Flower color variants of Flabébé are represented by the shared base species. Kalos uses this engine’s sampling rate of 20; it is not an imported XY encounter rate. Coastal fishing respects source rod weights by default; the existing fishing skill system still controls rod access and overrides Super Rod weights as in other regions.
- `python tools/import-kalos.py /path/to/csv-directory` regenerates the snapshot from `kalos-pokemon_species_names.csv`, `kalos-move_names.csv`, `kalos-pokemon_moves.csv`, `kalos-encounter_methods.csv`, `kalos-encounter_slots.csv` and `kalos-xy_encounters.csv` (upstream `encounters.csv`) and `kalos-pokemon.csv`. Download from the pinned repository’s `pokedex/data/csv/` directory. Source file hashes are retained. The importer also reads `kalos-pokemon.csv` to resolve alternate-form encounter IDs, preserving their original IDs in the snapshot. Basculin stripes and Pumpkaboo size slots currently use the shared base species display/stats.
- `tools/data/kalos-cyllage.json`, `tools/data/kalos-lumiose.json` and `tools/data/kalos-finale.json` and `tools/data/kalos-postgame.json` transcribe ordinary trainer rosters from the linked Bulbapedia walkthroughs. Story teams, partner moves/abilities, gifts, horde composition and story order are curated from the references recorded in `data/kalos.js`. Unspecified field trainer moves use the imported X/Y learnset. Generic dialogue, prize scaling and trainer IVs use project defaults. The gift/loan Lucario uses project IV/nature defaults; its level, moves, ability and held stone follow the story team. This is not an extracted XY script interpreter. Agnes’s three Burmy cloaks currently share the base Burmy display/stats; the roster snapshot retains the cloak identities.
- `tools/data/kalos-maison.json` contains the Super sets, Chatelaine teams, CPU partners and reference hashes. `python tools/import-kalos-maison.py /path/to/text-snapshots` rebuilds it from saved Bulbapedia HTML pages or the plain table snapshots listed in the importer. Source empty move slots are omitted when building battle sets.
- `python tools/import-kalos-cries.py` imports cries 650–718 from pinned PokeAPI/cries commit `b3a9044bb839b5e3152aaa536c7b463a0fcf0140`, with hashes. Older species retain the shared audio resources.
- `npm run build:kalos` rebuilds maps, the regional Town Map and `data/kalos.js` offline. Layouts, spiderweb, cliffs, mirrors, skate park, rope course, silhouette quiz floors and fireworks are authored 2D adaptations, not extracted 3DS geometry. Existing FireRed tiles/music, B2/W2 actors, follower sheets and generic trainer portraits are temporary stand-ins. Gym climbing uses native platform transfers rather than requiring the unrelated Rock Climb HM.

## Battle Chateau and Lumiose side activities

The Route 7 Battle Chateau has a reception and noble salon. Register, challenge arriving guests and earn the six titles from Baron through Grand Duke. Source thresholds are 0/5/30/100/300/1000 nobility points, with a 4095-point maximum. The offline catalog contains 83 source roster entries, including alternate Gym Leader teams, the Elite Four and Diantha. Story opponents require their original victories and the appropriate title. Guest capacity grows from six to ten. Invitation/Silver writs accelerate arrivals, Gold increases winnings, and Challenge/Blue/Red/Black writs change opponent levels; compatible effects stack and expire at midnight. The schedule uses the shared game's step clock, replacing the original real-time clock. Defeated guest appearances cannot award points twice. Generic moves, abilities and portraits remain project defaults where the source table does not specify them.

Two shopping/activity arcades connect to South and North Lumiose. Their door placement is an authored 2D arrangement, with the original boulevard story gates retained. Hotel Richissime's reception now opens when northern Lumiose opens; the Looker investigation's suite/lab gates remain unchanged.

- **Restaurants:** Le Nah, Le Yeah, Le Wow and Sushi High Roller provide all nine meal/format choices, with 17 source course rosters including moves, abilities and held items. Fees, turn targets and perfect-meal mushroom/Big Nugget rewards follow X/Y. Parties heal between won courses. Deviations from the target turn counts and fainted party Pokémon reduce rewards. Each menu records clears, best rewards and a perfect-meal star. A paid meal can resume between courses; reloading during a battle forfeits that meal. Full-Bag rewards wait safely for collection. Le Wow requires Champion status; Sushi additionally requires 90 style. Style discounts apply at each restaurant's source threshold. Native battle move-learning prompts can still occur between courses; the original 3DS course presentation and tipping scenes are not reproduced.
- **Hotel work:** Room-service memory orders, four bed-making stops and lost-and-found searches each allow one assignment per game day. Perfect jobs unlock four work levels at 0/5/15/30 perfect assignments, with source pay brackets through ¥50,000. Assignments and completed stops save independently. Bed efficiency uses walking-clock steps rather than wall-clock seconds; hotel dialogue and searches are local puzzles, not the original DS minigame scripts.
- **Juice Shoppe:** Independently buy a drink and blend two Berries each day. The saved daily menu grows with style. All 65 source Berry color/group classifications, friendship/EV recipe strengths, Rare/Ultra Rare Soda combinations and Perilous Soup are supported. EV/level caps are enforced before charging or consuming Berries. Sodas follow the source rule of granting levels without move-learning or evolution prompts.
- **Other services:** Loto-ID checks party and PC Trainer IDs, with saved daily numbers and capacity-safe prizes. The Stone Emporium and Poké Ball Boutique use the shared shop system. Galettes have a daily purchase, museum visits increase local style, and the Press reviews activities. Taxis use an announced flat fare between open boulevards. The PR studio saves a greeting/pose/appearance card; it is a local substitute for the original video editor and online sharing. Café Rouleau records skating lessons and unlocks the Lost Hotel master's Cosmic Flip lesson; original analog trick controls remain unimplemented.
- **Character appearance:** All **307 X/Y catalogue pieces** are included: 102 from the Calem collection and 205 from the Serena collection. The shared custom protagonist can wear either collection. Hats, shirts, pants/skirts, dresses, socks, shoes, bags and hat accessories have independent ownership and equipment slots. Source names, colors, prices, tags, boutique locations and seven-day stock schedules are preserved. Santalune, Cyllage, Laverre, Anistar and Snowbelle each have a boutique; Lumiose uses Boutique Couture, which requires 25 style. Starting wardrobes contain the 17 source default pieces; 288 pieces are purchasable and two are artist gifts. Wear at least three Colorful pieces for the Bamboo Sprig Hat, or three Feminine pieces for the Sundae Dress, then speak to Kiloude's artist. The artist awards each collection's piece once.
- **Shopping and fitting rooms:** Browse today's stock or the full catalogue, preview any piece without spending money, build a purchase basket, and explicitly buy or wear selected owned pieces. A Discount Coupon, when owned, halves a single basket transaction and is consumed only on a successful purchase; this update does not invent a replacement for its original online distribution. Dresses replace shirt/pants equipment and restore the underlying pieces when a top or bottom is worn again. Socks and hat accessories can be removed; the other core slots stay equipped. Save up to twelve named outfits and switch between them. Boutiques, home and Kalos Pokémon Centers provide wardrobe access. Clothing themes follow the source tag/tie rules. Old paid palette outfits remain available under Color outfits; hair colors, skin tone and existing purchases survive migration unchanged.
- **Clothing artwork:** Individual garments have authored pixel layers for the existing protagonist, including hat silhouettes, patterned tops/bottoms, skirts/dresses, footwear, bags and hat accessories. They appear in the fitting-room portrait, walking/running/action sheets, battle back sprite and Trainer-card portrait. This is a 2D interpretation of the complete catalogue, not a port of the original 3DS models or animations. Some fine garment details cannot be distinguished at the small overworld resolution. Wes retains his own appearance. Palette rendering needs readable image pixels, as provided by the local HTTP server; file-origin restrictions may retain the original appearance. Coiffure Clips' hair colors and free skin-tone changes remain available; original X/Y hairstyle geometry is still separate work.
- **Furfrou:** Friseur Furfrou applies all nine cosmetic trims for ¥500. Imported front/back/shiny form sprites appear in party displays and battles, with unchanged stats and the base party icon/follower. Trims expire after five game days. The source's progressive trim unlocks and PC-deposit reset are not reproduced.

## Exploration expansion

Route 22 now has its six early source Trainers, a late waterfall basin with the two source Ace Trainers, its original Lv.25–27 yellow-flower pool, additional treasure spots and a gate to Victory Road. The lower basin requires Wulfric's Badge/Waterfall progression; the League gate requires all eight Kalos Badges. Existing Route 22/Chamber of Emptiness maps remain valid for old saves. Entrances, NPC approaches and Fly arrivals are checked against the complete map graph.

Later-route hordes now cover Routes 7, 8, 10–12, 14–16 and 18–21, Frost Cavern and Terminus Cave, including mixed groups and the Houndour/Electrike and Aron/Larvitar version differences. Honey and normal walking encounters use these tables through the existing native five-Pokémon battle engine. The project retains its existing 1-in-20 horde sampling and 5% per-member Hidden Ability roll. Nidoran mixed-sex variants split the source encounter weight evenly. The three source trades add Bunnelby → Farfetch’d in Santalune, Luvdisc → Steelix in Cyllage and Jigglypuff → Bisharp in Snowbelle. Received level matches the offered Pokémon; nicknames and held items are retained, while OT details use project defaults. Trades require confirmation and cannot repeat.

Lost Hotel has one daily bin and three of four additional shaking bins on Tuesdays, with saved contents and used-bin records. Daily encounters include Trubbish/Garbodor; Tuesday encounters include Rotom and its five appliance forms. Source item pools are included, with a project sampling split between items and Pokémon. Full storage or a full Bag leaves the find waiting; reloading does not reroll contents. Additional treasure locations are authored placements rather than an exhaustive import of every source hidden item.

Rebuild activity rosters using `python tools/import-kalos-activities.py /path/to/plain-table-snapshots`. The snapshots and hashes are described in `tools/data/kalos-activities.json`; HTML-to-table normalization follows the Maison importer. `tools/data/kalos-exploration.json` records curated horde/flower tables and source hashes. `python tools/import-kalos-juice.py /path/to/Juice_Shoppe.html` imports Berry groups. `python tools/import-kalos-trims.py` imports the 36 form sprites from its pinned PokeAPI snapshot; follow it with `npm run build:sprite-metrics`. Run `npm run build:kalos` to rebuild locations and the regional data offline.
