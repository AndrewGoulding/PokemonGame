# Anville Town and the Green Line

Anville Town and its three source houses are connected to Gear Station by a
reciprocal Green Line platform warp. The town has a safe visited Fly destination,
source residents/dialogue and an authored 2D railway turntable. No Pokémon Center
is added to a town that has none in the source game.

The missing-Pansage quest starts with its owner in Anville, continues with the
Depot Agent and Pansage in Nimbasa, and ends with one BrightPowder. The reward
remains available if its Bag stack is full. Pansage is seen, not received.

Weekend collectors exchange two Escape Ropes for one Revive, one Star Piece for
one PP Up, or twenty Poké Balls for one Full Restore. They are absent on weekdays
and Hyperspeed Train days. Insufficient materials, canceled trades and full
reward stacks never consume the offered items.

The southeastern Depot Agent initially holds one Rare Candy. Completing a full
seven-win Subway set adds one to the pending lost-and-found quantity, capped at
ten. This uses the normal facility completion path for Single, Double and Multi
lines, including Super lines. Losses, cancellations and PWT rounds do not count.
After collecting, the next reward is rolled once from the ten source items and
saved until the next Subway success. Full Bags preserve both item and quantity.
Existing pre-import facility records are not converted into invented claims.

The worker explains the source train shown on the current game date. The monthly
pattern and five future-train dates follow the Anville reference; the game's
existing calendar adaptation has thirty days per month. The invisible Hyperspeed
Train appears on the 8th/18th/28th. Ten actual train explanations correspond to
source flags `0x0a76`–`0x0a7f` and earn Rail Enthusiast; the invisible train does
not inflate that count. Train art is authored pixel art with variant colors and
steam/future details, not a rendering of the DS building models. The flute's
original proximity-driven music layer is still outside the current audio scope.

Primary references: `res/field/scripts/anville_town*.s`, `nimbasa_city.s`,
`global_9600.s`, the corresponding event/text banks and pinned pokebw2 items.
The Subway lost-and-found behavior and calendar are cross-checked against
[Bulbapedia's Anville Town reference](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Anville_Town).
